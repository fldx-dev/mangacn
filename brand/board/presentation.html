The brief

A shadcn registry of manga-styled React components: rough.js ink outlines, screentone greys, one spot red, speech bubbles and sound effects. The logo should look drawn by the same pen as the components.

Speech

A hand-inked speech bubble that says one word: m.

  • Drawn with the same rough.js pen and Dela Gothic letters as the components
  • The speech bubble is manga's most recognisable device and mangacn's flagship component
  • Ink, paper and one spot red, taken straight from the theme tokens
  • A heavier small-size cut keeps the bubble and the m readable at 16 px
