Stock's sign-up page in ink, with stock's two files and exports: one add writes `app/signup/page.tsx` and `components/signup-form.tsx` where shadcn's signup-01 puts them, and its "Sign in" link goes to `/login`, where [Login 01](/docs/blocks/login-01) sits. The form is mangacn's card at the heavy outline width, its title in the display face under one spot <span lang="ja">登録</span> (registration) stamp; `SignupForm` takes the card's props, so it has no props table of its own.

## Props

`SignupForm` takes its root card's props. Every field submits under its `id` as its `name` (`name`, `email`, `password`, `confirm-password`). The copy is props too, each defaulting to what the preview shows: `title` (the heading, which names the form), `description`, `providers` (the other providers' buttons), and `footer`, the sign-in line ("Already have an account?" and a link to `/login`). `providers` takes nodes, so pass your own buttons wired to your sign-in calls (a client component of yours, since the page is a server component), or `null` for none.
