Stock's centred login card in ink, with stock's two files and exports: the studio's mark above a card at the heavy outline on the first screentone (tone-1, stock's muted ground), Apple and Google first with stock's marks in the current ink colour, then email and password, and the terms below. ## Props

`LoginForm` takes its root `div`'s props and `forgotPasswordHref`. No mangacn item installs a password-reset page, so the "Forgot your password?" link is drawn only when the prop is set: point it at yours with `<LoginForm forgotPasswordHref="/forgot-password" />` in `app/(mangacn-examples)/examples/login-03/page.tsx`. Every field submits under its `id` as its `name` (`email`, `password`).
