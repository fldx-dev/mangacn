Stock's wide login card in ink, with stock's two files and exports: on the first screentone (tone-1, stock's muted ground), the form with the title in the display face and stock's Apple, Google and Meta marks in the current ink colour behind their screen-reader labels, and in the card's second column, where stock shows a placeholder photo, a manga panel the items draw: a [halftone background](/docs/components/halftone-background), [focus lines](/docs/components/focus-lines) and one [sound effect](/docs/components/sfx-text) in a [manga frame](/docs/components/manga-frame), hidden from screen readers and shown from the `md` breakpoint up. Both files are server components; it is an alternative to the other login blocks, which write the same `app/login/page.tsx` and `components/login-form.tsx`, so adding one replaces the other.

## Props

`LoginForm` takes its root `div`'s props and `forgotPasswordHref`. No mangacn item installs a password-reset page, so the "Forgot your password?" link is drawn only when the prop is set: point it at yours with `<LoginForm forgotPasswordHref="/forgot-password" />` in `app/login/page.tsx`. Every field submits under its `id` as its `name` (`email`, `password`).

The copy is props too, each defaulting to what the preview shows: `title` (the heading, which names the form), `description`, `providers` (the other providers' buttons), and `footer`, the sign-up line ("Don't have an account?" and a link to `/signup`). `providers` takes nodes, so pass your own buttons wired to your sign-in calls (a client component of yours, since the page is a server component), or `null` for none, which also drops the "Or continue with" rule.
