Stock's file-tree sidebar in ink, with stock's two files and exports: one add writes `app/dashboard/page.tsx` and `components/app-sidebar.tsx`, where shadcn's sidebar-11 puts them. Mangacn's [Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar) lists a chapter's changed files with their state, then its folders as [collapsibles](/docs/components/collapsible), opening on the page being drawn; it collapses with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> on a Mac) or the trigger and opens as a sheet on phones, beside the page's blank [panels](/docs/components/panel). It is an alternative to the other sidebar blocks, so adding it replaces their shared files; swap the sample tree in `app-sidebar.tsx` for your own.

The page is a server component and `app-sidebar.tsx` is a client component, as in stock's block.
