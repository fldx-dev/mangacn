A series' volumes as a shelf of covers, each one link to its volume's page. Put it on the series page above [Chapter List](/docs/blocks/chapter-list), or on its own as the series' volume index.

It is a server component that composes panel-grid, aspect-ratio, manga-frame, page-number and stamp and draws nothing of its own. panel-grid lays the covers out, two a row on phones, three from `sm` and four from `md` by default (`columns` takes a count or one per breakpoint, as panel-grid does); each cover is a manga-frame filling a 2:3 aspect-ratio box, the shape of a tankōbon. Under it the volume number is a folio in the display face, beside its name ("Volume 3", or <span lang="ja">第3巻</span> with `numerals="ja"`), the title and the date as given. `isNew` stamps NEW (<span lang="ja">最新</span>) on the cover's corner. The text sits beside the folio, or under it when a cover is too narrow for both, and the title steps down a size on narrow covers.

The grid is a `<nav>` named by `label` around a list. Each volume is one link, cover, folio and text together, named by the volume and its title through `aria-labelledby` ("Volume 3 Night Market", with "NEW" after a new one); the folio repeats the number for the eye and is hidden from assistive technology, and each cover keeps its own `alt`. The covers in the first row load at once and the rest with `loading="lazy"`; the first row is the widest one `columns` gives.
