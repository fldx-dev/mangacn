Stock's centred sign-up card in ink, with stock's two files and exports: the studio's mark above a card at the heavy outline on the first screentone (tone-1, stock's muted ground), the title in the display face, name, email and the two passwords side by side, and the terms below. Both files are server components; it is an alternative to the other sign-up blocks, which write the same `app/signup/page.tsx` and `components/signup-form.tsx`, so adding one replaces the other.

## Props

`SignupForm` takes its root `div`'s props. Every field submits under its `id` as its `name` (`name`, `email`, `password`, `confirm-password`). The copy is props too, each defaulting to what the preview shows: `title` (the heading, which names the form), `description`, and `footer`, the sign-in line ("Already have an account?" and a link to `/login`).
