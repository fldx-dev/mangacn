Stock's notes-app shell in ink, with stock's eight files and exports: one add writes `app/dashboard/page.tsx` and `app-sidebar`, `nav-actions`, `nav-favorites`, `nav-main`, `nav-secondary`, `nav-workspaces` and `team-switcher` in `components/`, where shadcn's sidebar-10 puts them. Mangacn's [Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar) holds a manga studio's notebook: a studio switcher, the main links, favourite notes with their menus and workspaces whose pages open from a chevron; it collapses with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> on a Mac) or the trigger and opens as a sheet on phones. The header carries the note's actions in a [popover](/docs/components/popover), which opens from its button where stock opens it as the page loads. It is an alternative to the other sidebar blocks, so adding it replaces their shared files; swap the notebook in `app-sidebar.tsx` for your own.

The page is a server component and the seven component files are client components, as in stock's block.
