Stock's login page in ink, with stock's two files and exports: one add writes `app/login/page.tsx` and `components/login-form.tsx` where shadcn's login-01 puts them, and the page imports the form from `@/components/login-form`. The form sits on mangacn's card at the heavy outline width, its title in the display face under one spot <span lang="ja">受付</span> (reception) stamp, and its "Sign up" link goes to `/signup`, where [Signup 01](/docs/blocks/signup-01) sits.

## Props

`LoginForm` takes its root `div`'s props and `forgotPasswordHref`. No mangacn item installs a password-reset page, so the "Forgot your password?" link is drawn only when the prop is set: point it at yours with `<LoginForm forgotPasswordHref="/forgot-password" />` in `app/login/page.tsx`. Every field submits under its `id` as its `name` (`email`, `password`).

The copy is props too, each defaulting to what the preview shows: `title` (the heading, which names the form), `description`, `providers` (the other providers' buttons), and `footer`, the sign-up line ("Don't have an account?" and a link to `/signup`). `providers` takes nodes, so pass your own buttons wired to your sign-in calls (a client component of yours, since the page is a server component), or `null` for none.
