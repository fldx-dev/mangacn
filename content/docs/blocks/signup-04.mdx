Stock's wide sign-up card in ink, with stock's two files and exports: on the first screentone (tone-1, stock's muted ground), the form with the title in the display face and stock's Apple, Google and Meta marks in the current ink colour behind their screen-reader labels, and in the card's second column, where stock shows a placeholder photo, a manga panel the items draw: a [halftone background](/docs/components/halftone-background), [focus lines](/docs/components/focus-lines) and one [sound effect](/docs/components/sfx-text) in a [manga frame](/docs/components/manga-frame), hidden from screen readers and shown from the `md` breakpoint up. Both files are server components; it is an alternative to the other sign-up blocks, which write the same `app/signup/page.tsx` and `components/signup-form.tsx`, so adding one replaces the other.
