Stock's split-screen login page in ink, with stock's two files and exports: the studio's mark and the form on the left, the title in the display face, and on the right, where stock shows a placeholder photo, a manga panel the items draw: a [halftone background](/docs/components/halftone-background), [focus lines](/docs/components/focus-lines) and one [sound effect](/docs/components/sfx-text) in a heavy [manga frame](/docs/components/manga-frame). The panel is decoration, hidden from screen readers, and keeps stock's place: it shows from the `lg` breakpoint up and follows the theme in dark mode. It is an alternative to the other login blocks, which write the same `app/login/page.tsx` and `components/login-form.tsx`, so adding one replaces the other.
