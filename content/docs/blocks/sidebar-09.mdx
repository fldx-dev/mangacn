Stock's nested sidebars, mail-style, in ink, with stock's three files and exports: one add writes `app/(mangacn-examples)/examples/sidebar-09/page.tsx` and `app-sidebar` and `nav-user` in `components/` (the page as an example route, the components where shadcn's sidebar-09 puts them). Inside one mangacn [Sidebar](/docs/components/sidebar) sit two: an icon rail of mailboxes and, from the `md` breakpoint up, the open mailbox's letters with an Unreads [switch](/docs/components/switch) and a search field. Each is a navigation landmark with its own name ("Mailboxes", and the open mailbox's "… messages"), the whole collapses to the rail with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> on a Mac) or the trigger and opens as a sheet on phones, and choosing a mailbox opens it again. It is an alternative to the other sidebar blocks, so adding it replaces their shared files; swap the studio's letters in `app-sidebar.tsx` for your own.

The page is a server component and `app-sidebar.tsx` and `nav-user.tsx` are client components, as in stock's block.
