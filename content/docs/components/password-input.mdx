For sign-in and sign-up forms: the toggle lets people check what they typed, and `showStrength` adds a meter under the field when they choose a new password. Put it inside Field; pass `autoComplete="current-password"` or `"new-password"` so password managers fill it. `getPasswordStrength` is exported for your own checks; pass `strength` to swap in a stronger scorer.

The toggle follows the WAI-ARIA Button pattern as a toggle button (`aria-pressed`, `aria-controls` the input); the meter follows the Meter pattern, its word both visible and its `aria-valuetext`.

- Tab: moves from the input to the toggle.
- Enter or Space on the toggle: shows or hides the password; focus stays on the toggle.
