The stock form label in the body face, for a control that has no visible text of its own, such as a `checkbox`, an `input` or a `select`. `required` adds a spot asterisk; still set `required` on the control, which is what assistive tech announces.

## Examples

A label tied to a checkbox through `htmlFor`, so a click on the text toggles the box.
