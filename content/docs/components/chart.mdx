Charts drawn with recharts in ink: the container turns the axes and the cursor to ink and the grid to tone-3, the tooltip is a small heavy-outlined paper box and the legend sits in the body face. Series stay in ink, the tones and at most one spot, told apart by line weight, dash and Chart Tones' screentone fills rather than by hue; the theme's `--chart-1` to `--chart-5` are there for flat tones.

## Examples

Each chart has a fixed height and a caption, and its numbers are also in a visually hidden table beside it for screen readers.

Bars filled with Chart Tones' screentones: dense dots for print, a hatch for digital.
