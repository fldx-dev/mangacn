A band of sound effects sliding past between two sections of a page, as on the landing; give a word its own `color` for a spot-red hit or `lang` for katakana such as <span lang="ja">ドドド</span>. It is decoration, paused on hover and still under reduced motion, with a still copy for screen readers when you pass `label`; installing it adds the `mangacn-marquee` keyframes to your `globals.css`.
