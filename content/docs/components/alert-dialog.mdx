A confirmation that interrupts, such as deleting a chapter, on Dialog's ink overlay and heavy outline; pass `variant="destructive"` to the action when it cannot be undone, making it the one spot button on the surface. A long body scrolls in its own region so the actions stay on screen at phone width.
