A chat or comment thread in a fixed-height box that opens on the latest message, follows new ones and offers an outlined arrow button to jump back once the reader scrolls away. The scrolling is stock's from `@shadcn/react`; the viewport draws no outline of its own, so frame the box around it, and its scrollbar is a thin ink line.

## Examples

An editor's notes open at the latest one; scroll up and the jump button rises from the bottom edge.

<DemoFrame item="message-scroller" demo="default" />

A reading history opens at the first chapter with `defaultScrollPosition="start"`, with one jump button for each end.
