The narrator's voice, such as a time and place, a flashback or an unspoken thought (<span lang="ja">その頃……</span>), as real text in a square, heavily outlined box; pass `aria-label` to expose it as a note. With `corner` it pins to that corner of a Panel, outline on the frame; reach for it over the Panel's own `caption` when a panel needs two captions, a tone fill or the body face.
