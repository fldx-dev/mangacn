The narrator's voice: a time and place, a flashback, a thought nobody says out loud. It is real text in a square, heavily outlined box, in the lettering face by default. On its own it sits in the flow; with `corner` it pins itself to that corner of its nearest positioned parent, so inside a Panel its outline lies on the panel's frame, as the panel's own `caption` does. Reach for it over that prop when a panel needs two captions, a tone fill or the body face. Pass `aria-label` (the narrator) to expose it as a note, and put Japanese narration under `lang="ja"`.
