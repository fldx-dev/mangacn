Short notices that come and go, such as a saved bookmark or a failed page load, as paper cards with the heavy outline and ink glyphs for `success`, `info`, `warning` and `error` (`richColors` turns only errors spot red). Render `<Toaster />` once near your app's root and call `toast()` from `sonner` anywhere; it follows your theme through `next-themes`, as stock does.
