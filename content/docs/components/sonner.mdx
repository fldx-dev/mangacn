Short notices that come and go without interrupting, such as a saved bookmark or a failed page load. Render `<Toaster />` once near the root of your app, then call `toast()` from `sonner` anywhere; every toast is a paper card with the heavy outline, and `toast.success`, `info`, `warning` and `error` get an ink glyph. With `richColors` only errors change colour, to spot red. The Toaster follows your site theme through `next-themes`, as stock does; the examples run in their own stage.
