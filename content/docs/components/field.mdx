The parts of a form field around any control: label, description, error, and fieldsets with a display-face legend, so forms read alike whatever the control. A field is not a group: the input, select or checkbox inside keeps its own frame; set `data-invalid` on the field and `aria-invalid` on the control, and `FieldError` announces the message and inks a spot underline under the control.
