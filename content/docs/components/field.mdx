The parts of a form field around any control (label, description and error) and fieldsets with a display-face legend, so forms read alike whatever the control. A field is not a group: the input, select or checkbox inside it keeps its own frame.

## Examples

A described field above an invalid one. Set `data-invalid` on the field and `aria-invalid` on the control; `FieldError` announces the message and inks a spot underline under the control.
