Pick one colour, such as a theme's spot or a label's tint: a swatch button opens Popover's heavy-outlined paper with a saturation and brightness area, hue and alpha on [Slider](/docs/components/slider), a hex field and optional preset swatches. The colours are the user's own; every outline, thumb and focus ring around them is ink. Use `ColorPickerPanel` to place the panel inline, and Field to label it.

The area follows no WAI-ARIA pattern of its own: it is a focusable 2D slider. The rest follow the Dialog, Slider and Radio Group patterns:

- Enter or Space on the trigger opens the panel; Escape closes it and returns focus to the trigger.
- In the area, Left and Right change saturation, Up and Down change brightness; hold Shift for steps of 10.
- The hue and alpha sliders move with the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End.
- The hex field commits on Enter or when it loses focus, and marks a bad value invalid.
- In the swatches, the arrow keys move to the next colour and pick it.
