Stars for a review form, or for showing a score in a list of volumes. Interactive, it goes inside Field labelled by a FieldLabel (`aria-labelledby`) and posts its value under `name`; with `readOnly` it is one image, named "Rated 4.5 out of 5". `step={0.5}` adds half stars.

Interactive, it follows the WAI-ARIA Radio Group pattern: one radio per value, one tab stop, and a pointer hover previews a value without choosing it.

- Right / Up: chooses the next value.
- Left / Down: chooses the previous value.
- Home / End: chooses the lowest / highest value.
- Space: chooses the focused value.

The interactive rating has no name of its own: give it one with a FieldLabel or `aria-label`.
