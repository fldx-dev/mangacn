A panel that slides in from an edge and swipes away, for a chapter picker or a quick form on a phone, with one heavy stroke along its open edge. `swipeDirection` picks the edge (`down` is the bottom) and `showSwipeHandle` adds an ink stroke to grab; use Sheet when the panel should not swipe.
