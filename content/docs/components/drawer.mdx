A panel that slides in from an edge and swipes away, for a chapter picker or a quick form on a phone; one heavy stroke runs along its open edge, round its corners. `swipeDirection` picks the edge (`down` is the bottom), `showSwipeHandle` adds the short ink stroke to grab. Use Sheet when the panel should not be swiped; the examples run in their own stage, like Dialog.
