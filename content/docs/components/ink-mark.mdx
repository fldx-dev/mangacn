A pen mark around a few words in running text: underline the line that matters, circle a name, strike a wrong guess, or highlight a phrase behind its glyphs. The words stay ordinary text and keep their place; the mark is an `aria-hidden` drawing laid over their line boxes, one mark per line when the phrase wraps, redrawn when the text reflows. Flip `show` to draw it on (it appears at once under reduced motion), and say in the words themselves what the mark means: it is decoration, like a highlighter, not a `<mark>`.
