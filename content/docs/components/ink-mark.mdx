A pen mark around a few words in running text: underline the line that matters, circle a name, strike a wrong guess, highlight a phrase; pair it with Ink Arrow to point at what the sentence is about. The words keep their place under an `aria-hidden` drawing that follows them across line breaks and draws on when `show` turns true (at once under reduced motion), so say in the words what the mark means.
