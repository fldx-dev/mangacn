Actions on the thing under the pointer, such as bookmarking or reporting the page a reader right-clicks or long-presses: the same heavy-outlined paper cut-out as Dropdown Menu, with the highlighted item in ink and a destructive one in spot. Checkbox, radio and submenu items keep the stock markup and keyboard behaviour.

## Examples

The examples use a Panel as the trigger area and open the menu on load so you can see it; in your app a right-click or a long-press opens it.
