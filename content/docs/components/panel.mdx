One manga panel: a heavy ink frame on paper that holds a scene, with an optional screentone, corner caption and hatched shadow. Set panels on a page with Panel Grid and fill them with Speech Bubble, SFX Text and Speed Lines.

The `caption` prop draws the same box as a Caption Box on a corner, in the dialogue face (`captionFont="body"` for the page's face), and keeps a line of room for it so the content starts below it. Place a Caption Box by hand instead when you need its tone, a label or a second caption, and give the content that room yourself.
