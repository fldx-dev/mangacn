A searchable list of actions for a reader or an editor, inline on a page or in `CommandDialog`: the palette is Dialog's heavy-outlined paper, the search row sits over a hand-drawn rule, and the selected row is ink with paper text. Group headings are set in the display face and shortcuts are Kbd's paper key caps; typing filters and the arrow keys and Enter work as in cmdk, and a ⌘K binding to open the dialog is yours to add.
