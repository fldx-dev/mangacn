Related actions in one hand-drawn strip with inked rules between them, such as chapter navigation or zoom controls, across or (`orientation="vertical"`) down. Name the group with `aria-label`; mangacn's buttons, inputs and select triggers inside it draw no frame of their own, and a group of groups leaves each nested group to draw its own strip.
