Related actions in one hand-drawn strip with inked rules between them, such as chapter navigation or zoom controls, across or (`orientation="vertical"`) down. Inside it, mangacn's buttons, inputs and select triggers draw no frame of their own, and a group nested in a group draws its own strip.

## Examples
