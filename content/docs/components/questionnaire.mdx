Questions asked one at a time, for a reader survey, a sign-up step or an agent waiting on a choice: each answer is an outlined paper row that turns ink once chosen, under an inked progress track. Navigation and the keyboard are stock's from `@shadcn/react` (arrows move between answers, letters or numbers choose, Enter goes on), and Previous, Skip, Next and Submit are Button.

`QuestionnaireInput`, the free-text answer, is restyled and tested but left out of the examples: with `@shadcn/react` 0.3.1 a questionnaire that holds one stops with "Maximum update depth exceeded" under `next dev`, with stock's own file too. A production build runs it without error.
