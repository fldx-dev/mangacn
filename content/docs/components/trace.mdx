An agent's steps hung off one hand-drawn ink line, each with a drawn dot: solid when done, pulsing while `running` (still under reduced motion), hollow while `pending` and spot on `error`. A step can open on its details with [Collapsible](/docs/components/collapsible), and a trace sits in a [Message](/docs/components/message) above the answer.
