A red hanko pressed onto the page: approved, complete, new. The word inside stays real text, so it is read and translated like any other; when the word is more of a picture than something to read, pass `aria-label` and the stamp becomes an image named by it. It takes only its own box, so to press it over a Card or Panel corner, make the wrapper `relative` and place the stamp `absolute` yourself, as the card example does. Japanese words go under `lang="ja"`.
