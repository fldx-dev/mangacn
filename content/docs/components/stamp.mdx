A red hanko pressed onto the page, such as approved, complete or <span lang="ja">完結</span>, with the word inside as real text (pass `aria-label` when it is more picture than word). It takes only its own box: to press it onto a Card corner, make the wrapper `relative` and place the stamp `absolute`, as the card example does.
