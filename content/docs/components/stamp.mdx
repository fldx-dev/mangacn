A red hanko pressed onto the page, such as approved, complete or <span lang="ja">完結</span>, with the word inside as real text (pass `aria-label` when it is more picture than word). It takes only its own box: to press it onto a Card corner, make the wrapper `relative` and place the stamp `absolute`, as the card example does. Press it on plain paper, never over screentone: the grain and the inner ring vanish into the dots. A word that would overrun the inner ring shrinks to fit inside it once the page loads; at `lg`, about 8 Latin letters or 4 kanji stay readable, and two words wrap.
