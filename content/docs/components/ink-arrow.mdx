A hand-drawn arrow for pointing at something on the page: the next chapter, a button to press first, a detail in a panel. Without a label it is decoration and hidden from assistive tech, so the thing it points at must say what it is; with a `label` the text sits at the tail and the arrow becomes an image named by it. Pair it with Ink Mark to annotate a sentence and its call to action alike.
