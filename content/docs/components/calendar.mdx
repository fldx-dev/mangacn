A month to pick a date or a range from, for a release date, a reading schedule or a convention's days: an outlined paper card with the caption in the display face, today underlined and the picked days filled in ink. Put it in a `popover` behind a `button` for a date field; inside a `card` or `popover` it drops its own outline and uses theirs.
