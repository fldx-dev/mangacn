The folio in a manga page's corner, `7` or `7 / 24` in the display face with an optional chapter mark, read as "Page 7 of 24"; the landing's whole-page section prints its folio with it. It does not place itself: give it `absolute` in a Panel or reader page and `side="left"` on a left-hand page, with a Japanese mark under `lang="ja"` (<span lang="ja">第12話</span>).
