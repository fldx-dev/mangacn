A menu of actions behind one Button, such as bookmarking, sharing or reporting a chapter: a heavy-outlined paper cut-out with the highlighted item in ink and a destructive one in spot. Checkbox and radio items, shortcuts and submenus keep the stock markup and keyboard behaviour; the examples run in their own stage, so the open menu does not cover this page.
