A menu of actions behind one button, such as bookmarking, sharing or reporting a chapter. The highlighted item is inked with paper text and a destructive item turns spot; checkbox and radio items, labels, groups, shortcuts and submenus keep the stock markup and keyboard behaviour. The examples run in their own stage, so the open menu does not cover this page.
