A time without a popover, for a release hour, a signing slot or a deadline: hours and minutes in one ink outline, typed or stepped, posting `"HH:mm"` as a native time input does. Put it in a `field` with a label and an error, or beside `date-range-picker` for a date and a time.

Each segment follows the WAI-ARIA [Spinbutton pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/spinbutton/):

- Up and Down step by one (minutes by `minuteStep`) and wrap around.
- Page Up and Page Down step by 3 hours or 10 minutes.
- Home and End go to the segment's first and last value.
- Digits fill the segment and move on once it is full; Backspace clears it.
- Left and Right move between segments; A and P set AM or PM.
