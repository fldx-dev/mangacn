Who is on a series, in a reading group or on a shared shelf, from a plain list of names. It
draws [Avatar](/docs/components/avatar)'s `AvatarGroup` for you and folds everyone past `max`
into a "+N" button; for a hand-built row, use `AvatarGroup` and `AvatarGroupCount` directly.

The stack is a named group (`"{n} people"` unless you pass `aria-label`). The "+N" button follows
the WAI-ARIA Tooltip pattern: its tooltip lists the hidden names.

- Tab: focuses the "+N" button and shows the tooltip.
- Escape: hides the tooltip.
