A record of what happened when, for a series' release history, a changelog or a production schedule: each event a dot on one hand-drawn line, inked when done, ringed when current. It is static and renders on the server; for steps someone moves through, use [Stepper](/docs/components/stepper).

It is an ordered list (`<ol>`) with no keyboard of its own:

- each date is a `<time dateTime>`;
- the current item has `aria-current="true"` and says "current" to screen readers;
- the dots and the line are hidden from assistive tech.
