Wrap a right-to-left page or section in `DirectionProvider` so Base UI's components (slider, tabs, menus) step, open and fill in the reading direction. It draws nothing, so set `dir="rtl"` on the element too: the attribute turns the text and mangacn's ink marks.

## Variants

An Arabic line with an ink underline under `dir="rtl"`, and a slider inside the provider that fills from the right and steps the other way under the arrow keys.

<VariantFrame item="direction" variant="default" />

The provider can wrap part of a page: here only the page scrubber of an English reader runs right to left, with page 1 on the right.
