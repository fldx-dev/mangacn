The top navigation of a manga site, with sections such as Series, Genres and Library that open a panel of links: the triggers are set in the display face and turn ink while their panel is open, and the panel is Dropdown Menu's heavy-outlined paper. Links in the panel take the same ink highlight as a menu row, and the arrow keys, Tab and Escape work as in stock.
