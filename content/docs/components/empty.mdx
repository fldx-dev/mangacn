The state a list or page shows before there is anything in it: a mark, a title in the display face, a line of help and the next action. Leave `EmptyMedia` empty to get an `emanata` sweat drop, or a sparkle in the `icon` tile, or pass your own mark.

## Examples

With `EmptyMedia` left empty, the mark is the sweat drop; one action sits under the description.

<DemoFrame item="empty" demo="default" />
`variant="icon"` sets the sparkle in a small tile, and the description holds a link instead of a button.
