For a count, a price or a percentage that people nudge rather than type: copies per order, a page number, a discount. Put it inside Field with a FieldLabel; `format` shows yen or percent through `Intl` while the value stays a plain number.

It follows the WAI-ARIA Spinbutton pattern as Base UI's NumberField implements it. The − and + steppers are named, stay out of the tab order and turn off at the bounds.

- Up / Down: adds or takes away `step`.
- Shift + Up / Down, PageUp / PageDown: adds or takes away `largeStep`.
- Home / End: jumps to `min` / `max`.
