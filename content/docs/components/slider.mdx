A hand-drawn rule with an ink range and paper thumbs, for a value on a scale such as screentone density or a page range to export; the theming page's pen controls are built from it. Pass `value` or `defaultValue` as an array (one entry per thumb) and name it with `aria-labelledby` pointing at its visible label.
