Where a page sits (series, volume, chapter), with hand-inked chevrons between the crumbs and the current page in the display face, which sets Japanese titles too: <span lang="ja">第12話</span>. Fold a long trail into `BreadcrumbEllipsis`, and pass `render` to `BreadcrumbLink` for your router's link.
