Shows where a page sits, such as series, volume and chapter, with hand-inked chevrons between the crumbs and the current page in the display face, which sets Japanese titles too (<span lang="ja">第12話</span>). Long trails wrap; fold the middle into `BreadcrumbEllipsis`, and pass `render` to `BreadcrumbLink` for your router's link.
