Ink flung across the page behind or beside content: a splash, a band of drips, a spray of dots. Every instance draws its own shape from its `seed` (a hash of its id unless you pass one), so a page of splatters never repeats, and the same seed always draws the same marks. It is decoration, hidden from assistive tech, and takes only its own box: put it in a `relative isolate` wrapper and place it `absolute` behind an SFX Text or a heading, as the example below does.
