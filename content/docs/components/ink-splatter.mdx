Ink flung behind or beside content (a splash, a band of drips, a spray of dots), drawn from its `seed` so no two on a page repeat and the same seed always draws the same marks. It is decoration hidden from assistive tech and takes only its own box: place it `absolute` in a `relative isolate` wrapper behind an SFX Text or a heading, as the last example does.
