A first and a last day in one control, for a convention's dates, a reading log or a report's span: an outlined button that shows the range and opens a popover with presets and Calendar's range view. Pair it with [Time Picker](/docs/components/time-picker) when the days need hours too, and put it in a `field` for a label and an error.

It follows the WAI-ARIA [Date Picker Dialog pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/examples/datepicker-dialog/):

- Enter, Space or Down on the trigger opens the popover, with focus on the picked day (or today).
- The arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, Home and End move through the calendar's days, as in Calendar.
- Picking the second day keeps the popover open, so the range can be redone; a preset sets the range and closes it.
- Escape closes the popover and returns focus to the trigger.

## Variants

A week picked; the popover shows two months side by side, one on a phone.

<VariantFrame item="date-range-picker" variant="default" />

Presets of your own: a reading log's "This week" and the month the last volume came out. `presets={[]}` hides the column.

<VariantFrame item="date-range-picker" variant="presets" />

In a booking form's `field`: submit with no range and the trigger turns invalid under the field's error. With `name`, the form posts `booth-from` and `booth-to` as `yyyy-MM-dd`.
