A row of menus across the top of a reader or an editor, such as Series, Chapter and View: one outlined paper strip with a hand-drawn rule between the triggers, set in the display face. Each menu is Dropdown Menu's popup, which installs with it, and the arrow keys move along the bar and into the menus as in stock.
