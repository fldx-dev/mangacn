An image in a hand-drawn ink frame, with aspect presets, a halftone mat and a caption, for cover art and illustrations. `alt` is required with `src`. For a title card or a lettered scene, pass children instead of `src`: they sit on paper in a box of the same aspect, in the theme's fonts and tokens, so they follow dark mode and stay real text. For a panel of story with captions and bubbles, use Panel.
